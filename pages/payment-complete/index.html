How to prepare

  1. 01

    Arrive ten minutes early

    We’ll welcome you, show you around and get everyone settled before the session begins.

  2. 02

    Dress for the workbench

    Wear comfortable clothes and closed shoes. Making can be dusty, inky or a little messy.

  3. 03

    Bring your curiosity

    No previous experience is expected. We provide the shared tools and materials for your workshop.

What to expect

A workshop here is practical, social and deliberately unhurried.

We start together with a short introduction to the tools, materials and the day’s process. Your facilitator will demonstrate the essentials, then stay close while you work at your own pace.

You’ll share a table with a small group, ask questions freely and learn from what other people try. Experiments and imperfect first attempts are part of the session—not something to hide.
