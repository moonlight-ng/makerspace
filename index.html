Use the space

Our space at 30 Ajasa Street, Lagos Island doubles as a venue for workshops and creative events.

Guidelines
  • Free design visits include access to our shared computer with design tools installed and a Codex subscription.
  • To host a workshop, tell us what you want to teach, your preferred date, group size and any tools you need.
  • For private hire, send your date, hours, guest count and setup so we can confirm availability and cost.
  • This is a shared building, so there may be other guests around.
  • We can’t promise 24/7 power, but a backup is available.
  • Street parking is limited. Shared transport or taxis work best.
  • Setup is yours to handle. Leave the space as you found it and take responsibility for your guests.
